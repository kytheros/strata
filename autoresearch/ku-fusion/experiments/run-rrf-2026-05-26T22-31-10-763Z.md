### Shipping posture

- `CONFIG.benchmark.kuFusion.mode` stays default `"off"` per the spec's invariant
- `STRATA_KU_FUSION_MODE=rrf` is the validated improvement path for KU-heavy workloads
- Recommend flipping default to `"rrf"` in a future release after broader validation across non-KU question types (current eval scope is KU-only by design; effect on temporal, single-session, etc. is unmeasured)

### Why RRF beat append

M1 (append) just tacks turn-lane sessions onto the end of the chunk-lane list — no reordering, no signal cross-pollination. The wider-net chunk lane (limit=100) re-ranking by RRF lets a turn-lane-favored session that was ranked, say, 35th in the chunk lane get *re-ranked* into the top-20 fed to the answer model. That's a structural advantage append can't replicate.
